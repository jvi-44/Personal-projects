1 · Freeze-out region (low T)

At very low temperature there is too little thermal energy (kT ≪ Ed) to ionize the donors, so most donor electrons remain bound ("frozen") to their phosphorus atoms.

  • Fermi level sits between the donor level and Ec, rising toward Ed as T → 0.
  • Electron density is thermally activated: n ∝ exp(−Ed / 2kT) at the lowest T, then exp(−Ed / kT) as the donors begin to ionize.
  • Conductivity falls rapidly with cooling; devices stop working as designed.
  • Heavily doped silicon (Nd above about 3·10¹⁸ cm⁻³) goes through the Mott transition and does not freeze out; this simple model ignores that effect.

2 · Extrinsic (saturation) region (mid T)

Once kT is enough to ionize practically every donor but not enough to excite many electrons across the 1.12 eV gap, n stays pinned at the doping level.

  • n ≈ Nd and p ≈ ni² / Nd (minority carriers).
  • Flat plateau in the plot; this is the operating range of nearly all silicon devices.
  • Mobility, not carrier density, now determines how conductivity varies with T.
  • The Fermi level drifts down toward mid-gap as T rises.

3 · Intrinsic region (high T)

At high temperature, thermally generated electron-hole pairs outnumber the dopants. The semiconductor behaves as if undoped.

  • n ≈ p ≈ ni = √(NcNv)·exp(−Eg / 2kT).
  • Fermi level approaches mid-gap; p-n junctions lose rectification.
  • Steep exponential increase of carriers with T; the onset temperature climbs with Nd, roughly 450 K for 10¹⁴ cm⁻³ and about 900 K for 10¹⁶ cm⁻³.

Model & equations

Nc = 2.8·10¹⁹ (T/300)^1.5 cm⁻³   Nv = 1.04·10¹⁹ (T/300)^1.5 cm⁻³
Eg(T) = 1.17 − 4.73·10⁻⁴ T² / (T + 636) eV (Varshni)
n = Nc·exp(−(Ec − EF)/kT)   p = Nv·exp(−(EF − Ev)/kT)
Nd⁺ = Nd / (1 + 2·exp((EF − Ed)/kT))   (donor degeneracy g = 2)
Charge neutrality: n = p + Nd⁺   (solved numerically for EF by bisection)

Region classification: intrinsic if p > 10 % of n; otherwise freeze-out if less than 90 % of donors are ionized; otherwise extrinsic. Boltzmann statistics are used, and the model is valid for nondegenerate doping.
