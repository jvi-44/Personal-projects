        Travel notes

        How the plan is built

        Auto sets & reps

        Every day has a band style: Strength (slow 4-second reps), Endurance (more reps, lighter band), Tempo (6-second reps), Pulse or Light. The planner picks the most rounds that fit inside each block and spreads the remaining time into rests, so every block ends exactly on the clock.

        Reps go up by one every two weeks (up to +4). Use the week buttons to move ahead.

        Tracking

        When you play a leg for most of its time, the flight log ticks it off for you. Tick or untick any leg yourself, and add your band, dumbbell weight and distances. All five legs ticked stamps the day as visited.

        Treadmill every day: 1.6 km warm-up in 10 minutes, then 3.2 km in 20 minutes, 3 miles in total.

        The workout sits inside your Sunshine morning: rise and brush at 05:00, workout 05:10–06:20, then journal, goals, shower, breakfast and reading until 07:20. Bands and dumbbells are 10 minutes each to make room.
