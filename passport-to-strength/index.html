        How the plan is built

        Auto sets & reps

        Every day has a band style: Strength (slow 4-second reps), Endurance (more reps, lighter band), Tempo (6-second reps) or Pulse. The planner picks the most rounds that fit inside each block, then spreads the remaining time into rests, so every block ends exactly on the clock.

        Reps go up by one every two weeks (up to +4). Use the week buttons to move ahead.

        Training every day

        Arms get bands daily, so the band style alternates heavy and light days. Sunday in Bali is the lighter day for Pilates and bands. If your arms feel sore, use a lighter band and keep the reps.

        Treadmill every day: 1.6 km warm-up in 10 minutes, then 3.2 km in 20 minutes, 3 miles in total. Both targets are about 9.7 km/h (6.0 mph).

        Sources

        Bands: 12 min Resistance Band Arm Workout by fitbymik (curls, overhead press L/R, tricep extension L/R).

        Neck: 10 min Posture Fix Workout by Hailey C. Follow along with all 8 steps.

        Dance: placeholder. Use any 10-minute dance workout on YouTube.
